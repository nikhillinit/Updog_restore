## Solution Architecture

Lanes and order. "Agent" means the executor drafts and verifies locally. The
owner ratifies, merges, approves, dispatches, and performs every provider and
environment action.

```
M0  this plan PR (agent drafts; owner merges)                          TRIP-2
R1  ADR-116 ratification record PR (agent drafts; owner ratifies)      TRIP-2  --+
K   Railway S4 under ADR-118 + F_1.3.8 G2 readings (owner)                     --+--> W
E   pre-window read-only evidence: G9, G6, G8, census (owner)                  --+
A   ADR-079 remaining-write review, fix PRs, closure (agent; owner)    TRIP-2  ...> W, V
W   0062 window: F_1.3.8 item 8, S5 to S7 (owner)
V   ninth vehicle, release, closeout: F_1.3.8 items 9 to 11 (owner; agent drafts)
P   #1715 repair: built now as a draft PR, merged only after V        TRIP-2
```

R1, K, E, and A start in parallel. W opens when R1, K, and E have closed and no
confirmed ADR-079 gap is open (Q5). A can continue through W. Before V capture,
merge A's closure evidence and required fixes. Before Phase A dispatch, verify
ADR-079 closure on the exact vehicle merge SHA under item 4.

The owner checks A's private ledger before opening W, before dispatching or
approving the apply, and before each V step through step 8. Check it again
immediately before each V `Production` approval that permits a provider
mutation, including Phase A worker deploy, Phase B staging, and Vercel
promotion. Record the release SHA, run ID, job, and check time for each
approval. A reports any new confirmed gap to the owner immediately.

Merge rules:

- M0 and R1 merge before W's exact-SHA hold opens (F_1.3.8 item 8 step 4). A
  merge after the rehearsal dispatch invalidates the rehearsal.
- No PR merges during the S6 exact-SHA hold. During the ADR-107 freeze, only the
  captured vehicle may merge (release step 3).
- If a gap is confirmed during W before apply approval, stop and close the
  window per F_1.3.8 item 8. Reject the waiting apply if one exists. After the
  window closes, merge the fix and restart under item 8 with fresh exact-SHA
  evidence. If apply is already running, establish its terminal outcome and
  follow item 8's reconciliation rule for any partial, ambiguous, or unknown
  outcome. After a successful apply and S7 close, merge any required fix before
  V capture.
- A gap confirmed after V capture stops the attempt. Reject any waiting V
  `Production` approval and withhold further approvals. A failed exact-SHA
  closure check also stops the attempt if tracked evidence needs repair. When
  the owner verifies the release is inactive and no release provider mutation
  occurred, record that the old capture was abandoned and end its merge freeze.
  Merge any required fix and refresh the affected ADR-079 proof before the next
  capture.
  - **Open vehicle:** update the same PR onto the fixed `main`, then capture
    again and rerun its original pull-request CI under ADR-107.
  - **Merged vehicle:** cut a new docs-only successor vehicle PR from the fixed
    `main`. Obtain its own pre-merge capture and current-head CI, then run fresh
    release proof on its merge SHA. ADR-107 cannot recapture the merged vehicle.
  - **Release active, mutation started, or state unknown:** stop for separately
    authorized reconciliation before ending the freeze or proceeding. Once
    reconciled, merge any required fix and refresh the affected ADR-079 proof. A
    continued release uses a new successor vehicle and fresh evidence. Recheck
    the 0062 receipt's validity and expiry before relying on it.
- P stays a draft PR with auto-merge off until V closes out (F_1.3.8 item 11,
  ADR-103 freeze lift).

Why this order:

- R1 before W: the 0062 route is ADR-115 as amended by ADR-116. It does not
  exist until ratification is recorded.
- K before W: F_1.3.8's reason stands. A second Railway failure then costs
  nothing on the schema side.
- A in parallel (Q5): start the review before W so its remaining work takes less
  time after apply. Production still lacks the Phase 1 fixes (row 15). The
  release requires the tracked closure proof on the exact candidate.
- E before W: F_1.3.8 item 8 already requires G9, G6, and G8 read-only results
  before step 1. Running them during R1 and K uses idle owner time. The census
  joins them because the release carries the F_1.3.9 guards and any A fixes.
- P after V: Q3. It never changes the release candidate or breaks a hold.

Deadlines from F_1.3.8 still bind: release step 8 must finish before the 0062
apply artifact expires (90 days after upload) and before the step 2 capture
artifact expires (30 days after upload).
