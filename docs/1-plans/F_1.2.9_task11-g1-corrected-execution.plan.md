## Overview

contaminated G1 approval, rebuild a genuine G1 successor from a clean baseline,

partner-only persona errors, pervasive inherited "fresh-context/no historical
authority" prose (539 invalid fresh-review claims), and 182 `writes +

review recommended DO NOT APPROVE on three "blocking" findings; independent
verification (2026-08-20) refuted all three — the red team verified the main
checkout instead of the child-f worktree the plan explicitly anchors. One
red-team finding survives and is adopted here: release gates must include
exact-SHA cold-CI evidence, not warm-local runs alone.

## Solution Architecture

Five sequential phases with strict lane separation and authority boundaries.
Contaminated evidence never reaches production gates: the rejected candidate's
bytes are preserved untouched for forensics, a successor is rebuilt from the
clean baseline, approval halts on the review-locator blocker until the
coordinator supplies locator or normalization authority, the funds failure is
diagnosed from captured evidence only, and merge evidence requires exact-SHA
cold-CI green. Default posture is deny: no push, mutation, dispatch, merge, or
deployment without explicit grant.
