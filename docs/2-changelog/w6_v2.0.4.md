## Changes

### V2 Catch-Up Allocation Parity (F_2.0.4)

Unifies GP catch-up arithmetic in both V2 waterfall engines behind one pure
Decimal leaf and closes tier-level split and availability conservation gaps.
This is an engine-internal conformance correction: the catch-up tier remains
unreachable on the admitted public derivation path, so public behavior and
component versions do not change.

- **Locked methodology**:
  `shared/lib/internal-economics/v2/catch-up-allocation-v2.ts` implements the
  ratified F_2.0.0 stopping rule `grossCatchUpDue = max(0, (c(G+L)-G)/(g-c))`,
  replacing the divergent `(c(openingPrefPaid+remaining)-G)/g` engine
  arithmetic.
- **Capped integer-unit budgets**: requested values retain six-decimal HALF_UP
  quantization, while physical availability is floored before capping through
  `decimalToCappedCents`. Return of capital, preferred return, and catch-up
  reconstruct the payable total before subtraction, preventing a binding
  half-micro cap from over-distributing proceeds.
- **Joint quantized split**: catch-up and carry quantize each tier total once,
  split GP/LP with one largest-remainder pass, and apportion those settled
  buckets to eligible partners. Emitted GP/LP buckets and per-partner
  distributions conserve the tier total exactly.
- **Cumulative profit state**: whole-fund tracks one fund-level G/L accumulator;
  deal-by-deal tracks one clean-opening accumulator per entitlement pool. Later
  tiers see profit granted by earlier tiers in the same run.
- **Opening-history safeguards**: whole-fund catch-up rejects nonzero
  `pari_passu` opening preferred history until GP/LP provenance exists.
  Deal-by-deal rejects nonzero scalar opening profit history at the engine
  boundary because it cannot assign that history to entitlement pools.
- **Defensive cohort invariants**: positive catch-up or carry GP/LP buckets with
  no eligible partner cohort throw instead of silently dropping value.
- **Coverage**: 30 new tests versus merge-base `e0ac8ac9d` — 15 leaf cases, 8
  deal-by-deal cases, and 7 whole-fund cases. Coverage includes locked-oracle
  discrimination, binding-cap flooring, half-micro cross-tier conservation,
  distinct-deal pool independence and order invariance, single-pool cross-engine
  parity, empty cohorts, and unsupported opening history.
- **Review follow-up**: canonical PR review found binding-cap over-distribution
  and unsupported `pari_passu` opening-history classification. Both were fixed
  before exact-head review. A later refactor consolidated split and
  partner-apportionment helpers without changing the locked formula or frozen
  surfaces.
