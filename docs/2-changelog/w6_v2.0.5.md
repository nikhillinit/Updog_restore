## Changes

### V2 F3b: Event-Stream Atomicity, Source-Lot Lineage, Eventful Receipt (F_2.0.5)

Closes the residual F3 scope recorded in F_2.0.2 section 8, engine-internal
under `shared/lib/internal-economics/v2/` and
`shared/contracts/internal-economics/`:

- **Whole-stream atomicity.** `processEventsV2ForTest` value-deep-clones the
  caller's `EventStreamState` (exhaustive per-key construction, compile-time
  error on un-cloned fields) and returns either the first refusal (caller state
  byte-untouched) or the fully applied staged state. Per-event order: capability
  check, `processCallableCommitment` (previously exported but never invoked —
  callable overrun streams now refuse), then event-specific validation and
  application.
- **Unified source-lot registry and lineage.** `cashSourceLots` becomes an
  origin-discriminated union seeded from opening provenance, admitting
  opening-lot-funded deployments (previously refused as unknown). Every lot
  insertion refuses on id collision. Staged consumption and partner-effect
  records retain which event consumed what; investment lots retain their
  validated funding vectors.
- **Eventful receipt 2.2.0.** Seven-account double-entry journal (posting-matrix
  rows per admitted event kind plus derivation-stage distribution postings from
  a pre-conversion tier-partner vector), lineage disclosure (per-lot consuming
  events, per-slice funding vectors), source-discriminated cash-flow entries,
  staged-derived partner capital figures (`calledCapital` no longer read from
  raw input), lane-correct waterfall manifest, and conservation extended across
  journal accounts. Output-row accounting counts nested lineage rows; ROC
  distributions that would overdraw a partner's unreturned capital fail closed.
- **Waterfall proceeds selection.** Both engines select realization lots by
  `origin === 'event' && sourceKind === 'realization_proceeds'` (immune to
  `proceeds:`-shaped opening ids) and distribute `remainingBalance` (fixes
  latent over-distribution of proceeds lots partially consumed by later
  deployments).
- **Dual-lane certification enabled.** `certifyInternalEconomicsDualLaneV2` runs
  both lanes behind a shared event-capability guard and returns both receipts;
  `deriveInternalEconomicsV2`'s admission guard is unchanged.
- **Code-review hardening (3 additional latent defects fixed):** contributions
  from undeclared partners now refuse (`SCHEMA_VALIDATION_FAILED`/settlement);
  realizations with relief rows from a different deal now refuse
  (`INVESTMENT_LOT_RELIEF_VIOLATION`/provenance); the reserve funding classifier
  excludes unclassified opening cash from both eligibility buckets.
- **Fixtures.** V2-S-0100's frozen 2.1.0 input and receipt literals are retained
  byte-frozen as historical certification; its live derivation and V2-S-0101
  transition to 2.2.0 through the changed-case manifest with economics proven
  identical (V2-S-0100) and single-reason hash change (both).

Version literals: receipt, serializer, event engine, composite implementation,
and both waterfall constants at 2.2.0; input contract and normalizer unchanged
at 2.0.1 (composite prefix intentionally split, ADR-090).

Parked (consumer-backed follow-ups, ADR-090): correction/write-off/conversion
policy (2B), persistence/queues (2C), ROC apportionment rule, per-security
proceeds identity within a deal.

No routes, migrations, schema files, workers, flags, or client code.
Conformance-level only; public derivation behavior unchanged. Merge establishes
eligibility only; production dispatch remains a separate owner-authorized action
under the governing policy.
