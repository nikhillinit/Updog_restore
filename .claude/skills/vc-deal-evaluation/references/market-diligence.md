# Market Diligence

Build a dated market evidence ledger from supplied materials. Keep market size,
growth, competitors, buyer behavior, and regulation separated by statement
class. Distinguish company assertions from independent observations.

Show credible conflicting sources side by side. Re-ground stale market facts or
limit conclusions to the dated evidence. Route legal interpretation or
compliance questions to counsel. Do not make provider calls or persist a market
record.
