# IC Red-Team

Review a supplied IC memo for unsupported observations, overextended inferences,
hidden assumptions, stale evidence, unresolved contradictions, missing
provenance, and decision leakage.

Return findings, severity, cited memo/evidence location, and the evidence or
question needed to resolve each finding. Do not rewrite the memo, approve or
reject an investment, or create an alternative recommendation. Human investment
authority remains required.
