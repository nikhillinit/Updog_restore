# Deal Screening

Produce a compact screen from supplied evidence: company, round, evidence
ledger, thesis fit, key risks, open questions, and next diligence request.

Use support bands only as evidence coverage, not investment approval:

| Band | Meaning                           | Outcome constraint                       |
| ---- | --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| A    | strong, current support           | may support a human-led advance decision |
| B    | meaningful but incomplete support | advance only with named diligence gaps   |
| C    | limited or conflicting support    | hold or decline pending evidence         |
| D    | weak support                      | never label PASS                         |

Do not replace unknowns with a score. Human investment authority remains
required.
