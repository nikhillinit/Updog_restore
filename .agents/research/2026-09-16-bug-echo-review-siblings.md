## Patterns (each from a fix or review finding that just shipped)

| Pattern                                        | Canon (fixed form)                                                                               | Anti-pattern                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Version-blind reuse of a persisted calculation | `metrics-engine.ts` puts `ENGINE_VERSION` in the inputs-hash preimage (#1526 round 2)            | a lookup or unique key on a content hash whose preimage omits the code version |
